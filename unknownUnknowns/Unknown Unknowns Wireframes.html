Color two ink levels, no accent

Where each appears

Paper — landing, settings, prompts, briefings, score reveal. Reading + composing.

Ink — countdown & recording. The "ritual" surface; phone becomes a microphone.

Regressions

No red. Down-deltas use the same weight as up-deltas, with a leading − and a small ▾. Honesty is delivered by typography, not alarm.

If we ever need an accent

Reserved black bar (8px) under the active line. Color stays out.

Motion deliberate, never decorative

  • Countdown 3→2→1→GO Each glyph hard-cuts in at 1.00s; on exit, scales 1.00→1.04 and fades over 200ms. No crossfade.
  • Prep ring (10s) Stroke fills clockwise, linear. At 0.0s the ring snaps to full, the dot scales 1.00→1.06, mic engages. No "tap to start".
  • Record pulse Dot scales 1.00↔1.03 at ~52 BPM (resting heart). Radial waveform driven by FFT bins, opacity 0.4→1.0 mapped to amplitude.
  • Briefing transition Page slides 60% from right, opacity 0→1, 360ms ease-out. Status dots tick in sync.
  • Score reveal Each row fades in over 180ms, 80ms stagger. Numbers count up over 600ms, ease-out. No bounce, no confetti, no haptics on regression.

The sky is blue not because air is blue, but because the air scatters short wavelengths more aggressively than long ones.
